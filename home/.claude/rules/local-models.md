# Local models: codenames, and adding one

- Every picker (Tailscode via opencode, omp) shows a codename: llama-swap `name`, one or two words, at most 16 characters, Title Case, derived from the full descriptor (`Bolt`, `Super`, `Pennyroyal`, `Glimmer`). Add one trait word only to tell siblings of a family apart (`Bolt Unchained` is the uncensored Bolt, `Qwen Daily` the default Qwen). Never put engine, quant or context in it.
- The full descriptor `Family Size · [Uncensored ·] Engine · Quant · Speculation · Context` (size is the total parameter count, binary K) goes in llama-swap `description` and in the model's blackwall `models.toml` `name`, which the leaderboard shows. `sync-local-models.py` warns when the two drift.
- Ids never change: blackwall's database and Tailscode's saved per-session models key on them. `metadata.agent.opencode_id` keeps an old opencode id (`sglang-27b` for `pennyroyal`).
- Adding a model: (1) llama-swap entry with `name`, `description` and `metadata.agent` (`style` qwen, muse, nemotron or gptoss; optional `opencode_id`, `opencode: false`, `context`, `vision: false`, `efforts`), (2) a blackwall `models.toml` entry with `name` = the descriptor, `hf_id` and `quantization`, (3) run `python3 ~/claudeconfig/scripts/sync-local-models.py` (`--check` previews). It regenerates the llama-server block of `~/.config/opencode/opencode.local.json` and `~/.omp/agent/models.yml`; never hand-edit those, a model without `metadata.agent` is not in either picker. (4) Commit `opencode.local.json` in `~/dotfiles`. (5) Tell Marcus to restart opencode-serve from the Tailscode app and omp-bridge; never restart either yourself.
